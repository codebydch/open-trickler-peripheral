Calibrate for a Powder

The trickler measures how this powder feeds — trickler 1 only, the powder measure never runs — and proposes final-approach settings for the fastest charge that lands heavy no more than one time in four. It primes the tube, finds the speed the motor stalls at, then pulses at every speed and length in the [calibration] grid, weighing each pulse and reading again a second later for what is still landing. About four minutes with the shipped grid. Put an empty cup on the pan and keep the hopper topped up.

Tuning by hand

The routine is not the only way. Set the values on the tuning page, throw a few charges, and judge them by the Landed column on the history page — what the pan weighed once the powder in the air came down, which is the number that matters. Save the settings to a profile from the tuning page when a powder is throwing well.
